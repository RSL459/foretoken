# Knowledge or Experience Title

English | [简体中文](guidance-template_zh.md)

Use this template for entries in `guidance/knowledge/` or `guidance/meta-knowledge/`, adapting the sections to the topic. See [recording and learning](../guidance/meta-knowledge/05-recording-and-learning.md) for turning research and experiments into reusable knowledge.

## Problem and idea

What problem does the entry address? What is the key finding or method? Explain the reason with an execution sequence, equations, or an example.

## Applicable conditions

Which models, workloads, hardware, versions, or implementations does it apply to? Explain the trade-offs, known issues, and hypotheses still to investigate.

## Evidence and implementation

Link original sources, relevant code, and experiment records. Identify findings reported by sources and those supported by measurements. Preserve useful input construction, experiment entrypoints, and execution instructions.

## Application and next steps

When should later work consult this entry, and how can it inform analysis or design? For inefficient approaches, explain how to recognize the situation early and avoid repeating the detour. State how far implementation and validation have progressed and what remains to investigate.
